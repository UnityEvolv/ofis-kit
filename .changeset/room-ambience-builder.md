Ambience controls in the builder (UO-279). Pass `ambienceLibrary` (`AmbienceTrack[]`) to `OfficeBuilder` or `BuilderSteps` and the template panel offers an office ambience, and each room's panel a choice of following the office, none, or its own loop, with a preview so the author hears it before publishing. Meeting and conference rooms get a word of guidance, not a refusal. Without a library the controls are not offered and a template's existing choices are kept.
