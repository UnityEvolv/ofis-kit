The padlock on a room's bar shows the door as it stands: open on an open room, closed on a locked one. It used to show what pressing would do, so the room you had just walked into carried a closed padlock and the lock looked as if it had followed you. The button's name is unchanged (Lock, Unlock). From inside a locked room the closed padlock on the button is the one sign of it; the separate lock mark is for people outside.
