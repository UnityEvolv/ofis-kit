The padlock is on every room that can be locked, for everybody: open on an open room, closed on a locked one. Until now an open room showed a padlock only to whoever stood in it, as their Lock button, so it turned up wherever a person walked and nobody else saw it. From outside the padlock is the room's state and nothing to press; inside it is still the Lock or Unlock button. A narrow bar leaves it out, as it does the head count.
