The list view on a phone's width keeps a room card's heading for the room's name and what is happening in it, and puts every control the room has (Join or Knock, the padlock, the host's actions and the menu) on one row along the bottom of the card, under the people. `RoomBar` takes `below`, `children` and `end` for it. Wider lists are unchanged.
